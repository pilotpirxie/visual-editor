About

We build Fieldnote so product teams never lose a customer insight

Fieldnote is an independent company of researchers, designers and engineers who still run customer interviews every week.

Start free trial Contact us

Our story

We started Fieldnote after losing one interview too many

In 2021 we were three product managers drowning in recordings. The insights were in there somewhere, but nobody had time to find them.

So we built a tool for ourselves: something that listened, took notes and remembered what every customer said.

Today Fieldnote helps more than 900 teams hear their customers. We are a remote team of 28 across Lisbon, Toronto and Singapore.

We still run every product decision past real customers, and we use Fieldnote to do it.
